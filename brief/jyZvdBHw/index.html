Animal Fact of the Day

The mimic octopus (*Thaumoctopus mimicus*), discovered off Sulawesi in 1998, is the only known animal that dynamically impersonates multiple other species—flattening itself and rippling along the seafloor to become a sole, trailing two arms behind it to pose as a banded sea snake, or spreading all arms to mimic a lionfish's venomous spines—apparently selecting which creature to imitate based on which predator is currently threatening it.
