2. Researcher finds Microsoft embeds server-issued GUIDs in AI images

Security researcher Xusheng Li reverse-engineered Microsoft Paint and Photos and found both encode a 128-bit server-issued GUID invisibly into the pixels of locally generated AI images, obtained when the user's prompt is sent to Microsoft for moderation. Paint fails the generation outright if the watermark cannot be applied; Photos returns the image anyway. Microsoft documents its C2PA metadata but has not explained the pixel-embedded GUID, which goes beyond the machine-readable marking required by EU AI Act Article 50.
