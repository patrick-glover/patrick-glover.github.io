Animal Fact of the Day

The mimic octopus (*Thaumoctopus mimicus*), discovered off Sulawesi in 1998, is the only known cephalopod that dynamically impersonates multiple other species—flattening itself and undulating two arms to look like a venomous sole, spreading all arms to mimic a lionfish's spines, or hiding in a burrow with just two banded arms extended to resemble a sea snake—and it appears to choose which animal to impersonate based on the specific predator threatening it.
