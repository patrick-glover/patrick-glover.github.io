Animal Fact of the Day

The mimic octopus (*Thaumoctopus mimicus*), discovered off the coast of Sulawesi in 1998, can dynamically impersonate at least 15 different species — including lionfish, flatfish, sea snakes, and jellyfish — by reshaping its body, altering its skin color and texture, and changing its swimming style on the fly, making it the only known animal that selectively chooses which species to mimic based on the specific predator threatening it.
