Animal Fact of the Day

The bar-tailed godwit (*Limosa lapponica*) holds the record for the longest nonstop flight of any bird, with one satellite-tagged individual flying continuously from Alaska to New Zealand — roughly 13,560 kilometers — over about 11 days without pausing to eat, drink, or sleep, a feat made possible in part by the bird's ability to shrink its digestive organs before departure to reduce weight and make room for extra fat reserves.
