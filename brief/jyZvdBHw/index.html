Animal Fact of the Day

The mimic octopus (*Thaumoctopus mimicus*), discovered off the coast of Sulawesi in 1998, can dynamically impersonate at least 15 different species — including lionfish, flatfish, and sea snakes — by reshaping its body, altering its skin texture and color, and modifying its swimming style on the fly, making it the only known animal to selectively choose which species to mimic based on the specific predator threatening it at that moment.
