Animal Fact of the Day

The mimic octopus (*Thaumoctopus mimicus*), discovered off Sulawesi in 1998, can dynamically impersonate at least 15 different species—including lionfish, flatfish, sea snakes, and jellyfish—by reshaping its body, altering its skin texture and color, and adjusting its swimming style on the fly, making it the only known animal to selectively choose which species to mimic based on which predator is currently threatening it.
