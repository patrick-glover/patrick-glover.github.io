Leaders of Nvidia, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and xAI signed the "White House Accord on Superintelligence," a voluntary pledge to police their own industry. Animal Fact of the Day

The wood frog (*Rana sylvatica*) survives the harsh North American winter by allowing up to 65% of its body water to freeze solid — its heart stops beating, its brain shows no activity, and it ceases breathing for weeks at a time, yet it thaws and hops away in spring thanks to a flood of glucose it pumps into its cells as a natural antifreeze, preventing ice crystals from destroying them from within.
