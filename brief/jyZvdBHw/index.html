Animal Fact of the Day

The mimic octopus (*Thaumoctopus mimicus*), discovered off Sulawesi in 1998, is the only known animal that dynamically impersonates multiple other species — it can reshape its body, alter its color and movement patterns to convincingly pose as at least 15 different animals, including lionfish, flatfish, sea snakes, and jellyfish, apparently choosing which species to mimic based on which predator is currently threatening it.
