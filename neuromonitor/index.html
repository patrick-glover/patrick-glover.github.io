3 · Measuring autoregulation

PRx finds the best CPP

PRx is a moving correlation of slow waves in MAP and ICP. Reactive vessels push back: MAP up, ICP down, PRx negative. Passive vessels transmit pressure: ICP follows MAP, PRx positive.

Plot PRx against CPP over hours and it forms a U. The bottom is CPPopt.
